Refund & Cancellation Policy

How refunds work for PDF and online tests purchased on Master Testing Agency.

1. Overview

Master Testing Agency ("we", "us", "our") sells digital PDF test papers and online mock tests ("Tests") for Class 10 CBSE, Class 12 CBSE, NEET (UG), JEE Main and JEE Advanced. Because Tests are digital products delivered instantly on successful payment, this policy explains when a purchase is, and is not, eligible for a refund.

2. General Rule for Digital Goods

Once a Test has been accessed — meaning a PDF has been downloaded, or an online test has been started — the purchase is treated as delivered and used, and is not eligible for a refund except as set out in Section 3.

3. When a Refund Is Available

  • Failed or duplicate payment. If an amount is debited from your card, UPI, or bank account but the Test is not unlocked in your library due to a payment or technical failure, you are entitled to a refund. Under RBI's Turn Around Time framework for failed transactions, such amounts are auto-reversed to your original payment method within 5 working days (T+5); if this does not happen, contact us immediately.
  • Wrong or duplicate purchase. If you accidentally bought the same Test twice, or purchased the wrong Test, and have not downloaded the PDF or started the online test, you may request a refund within 24 hours of purchase.
  • Technical defect. If a purchased Test is genuinely inaccessible, corrupted, or defective due to an error on our end, and our support team cannot resolve it within 3 business days of you reporting it, you are entitled to a full refund for that Test.

4. When a Refund Is Not Available

  • Once a PDF Test has been downloaded.
  • Once an online Test has been started — even if not completed or submitted.
  • Change of mind, or dissatisfaction with question difficulty, after access has been granted. Sample questions on the site let you check the format and difficulty before you buy.
  • Where the correct exam, subject, and chapter were clearly stated on the listing at the time of purchase.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email mastertestingagency@gmail.com from the address used at checkout, with your order or payment reference and a short description of the issue. We aim to acknowledge every request within 48 hours.

6. Refund Timeline & Mode

Approved refunds are processed to the original mode of payment (UPI, card, or netbanking) within 7–10 business days, subject to your bank's or payment provider's own processing times. We do not issue refunds to a different account, or as cash.

7. No Subscriptions

Master Testing Agency does not operate subscriptions — every purchase is a one-time payment for a specific Test or bundle. There is nothing to cancel on an ongoing basis; Sections 3 and 4 above govern what is refundable.
